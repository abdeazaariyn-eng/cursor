# مهد بيبي — Brand Summary

## Brand Name
**Arabic:** مهد بيبي
**Latin:** Mahd Baby
**Domain:** mahdbaby.shop

The word **مهد** (mahd) means *cradle / safe place to rest*. It's short, soft, easy to remember, and immediately category-clear without sounding like a generic baby store.

---

## Identity

Mahd Baby is a **curated boutique for new mothers in the Gulf** — not a marketplace, not a drop-shipper, not a catalog of every baby gadget on Alibaba.

We carry a **small, carefully chosen line** of mom-and-baby essentials. Every item is selected against three filters: **safety, real daily usefulness, and quality of materials**. If a product doesn't pass all three, it doesn't enter the store.

**Personality (how the brand "speaks"):**
- Calm, confident, knowledgeable — the older sister who already did the research
- Warm and respectful — speaks *to* the mom, not *at* her
- Honest about what we sell and what we don't — no medical claims, no fake urgency
- Gulf-native — we use Khaleeji tone, we ship from the region, we answer in Arabic

**Personality (what the brand is *not*):**
- Not a hype-driven dropshipping store
- Not a clinic — we don't make medical claims
- Not loud, neon, or exaggerated

---

## Positioning

> **For Gulf mothers who don't have time to vet every product themselves —**
> **Mahd Baby is the trusted, curated shortlist of essentials that are actually safe, actually useful, and actually worth the money.**
> **Unlike random baby stores, every item is hand-picked, SFDA-aligned, and backed by a 30-day no-questions guarantee with cash on delivery.**

### ICP (Ideal Customer)
- New or expecting mothers, 24–38 years old
- Lives in **Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman**
- Speaks Arabic at home, often bilingual
- Comfortable shopping online but **cautious about quality and counterfeits**
- Values **trust > price**, but not luxury — wants smart, safe spending
- Buys via **Instagram / TikTok discovery → website → COD checkout**

### Three Pillars of Trust (must appear on every page)
1. **اختيار مدروس** — Curated selection (not endless catalog)
2. **مطابق لمعايير SFDA** — SFDA-aligned medical-grade materials
3. **ضمان ذهبي 30 يوم + دفع عند الاستلام** — Risk-free for the customer

### Tone Rules for Copy
- Replace hype words (*رهيب، خرافي، خيال*) with calm authority (*مدروس، معتمد، موثوق*)
- Replace fake urgency (*الكميات على وشك النفاد!!*) with honest urgency (*طلبات اليوم تُشحن غداً*)
- Always reference at least one of the three trust pillars per section
- No medical claims — say *"يساعد على"* not *"يعالج"*

## Brand Color

### Signature Color — **Terracotta `#B97863`**

A warm, earthy, maternal tone. It's distinctive (not the typical pastel pink/blue baby palette), it conveys natural materials and warmth, and it's already the soul of our visual identity. Every primary CTA and brand mark uses this color.

### Supporting Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signature / Primary** | Terracotta | `#B97863` | CTAs, logo, highlights |
| **Authority / Text** | Espresso | `#2F2523` | Headings, footer, deep anchors |
| **Trust / Safety** | Sage | `#4F7F70` | SFDA badges, certifications, guarantees |
| **Premium accent** | Honey Gold | `#D9A441` | Award/guarantee marks, ratings |
| **Canvas** | Warm Ivory | `#FFF9F5` | Page background |
| **Soft surface** | Blush Cream | `#F7EDE8` | Section backgrounds, cards |
| **Body text** | Warm Brown | `#7B5E57` | Paragraph copy |

**Why this combination signals authority:**
- Terracotta + Espresso = boutique, intentional, not childish
- Sage on trust badges = medical/safety cue without screaming "hospital"
- Gold = premium without being flashy
- Cream canvas = calm, never overwhelming
